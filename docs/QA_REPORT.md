# QA report

The full QA before submission, run on 24 Nov 2026 as the user's QA brief asks: test, report, fix, re-test.

- **Production:** https://lila-player-journeys-psi.vercel.app, serving `main` at `abdaf02` (the merge of pull request #8).
- **Method:** Claude in Chrome on fresh tabs. Page state was read from the DOM and from the canvas pixels by scripts; clicks, keys, hovers and drags were real input from the browser tool wherever a row says so. Expected numbers were counted from the committed data files with a separate script, not taken from the app.
- **Previous audit:** a read-only audit on 24 Nov. Its results are reused where nothing changed; its open items are re-checked in Group C.
- **Working files:** `docs/BRIEF.md` (the brief as a checklist), the plan, the progress log and the audit were in the repo during the QA. They are the author's working files and are kept out of the submitted repo, so this report names them without links.
- **Evidence:** screenshots in [docs/qa/](qa/); numbers and texts as read from the page.
- **Result key:** PASS, PARTIAL, FAIL. "Observation" marks something that works but deserves a note.
- **Layout redesign, 25 Nov:** the layout was redesigned after this QA. Its own test record is the last section, [Layout redesign](#layout-redesign-25-nov).

## Summary

**The tool passes the brief.** Every core requirement works on production, and every number it shows equals a separate count from the data files. PageSpeed Insights scores it 90 / 100 / 100 / 100 on desktop and 95 / 100 / 100 / 100 on mobile, and playback runs at 59.8 frames a second. No blocker was found. The six should-fix items are fixed on `qa-fixes` (pull request [#9](https://github.com/YesudasZ/lila-player-journeys/pull/9)) and pass their re-test.

## Layout redesign (25 Nov)

On a laptop the left column stacked five sections, and the match list and the journeys of an open match showed two or three rows each. The redesign (branch `ux-layout`) keeps what you choose on the left, puts what you read on the map, and gives the open match a panel on the right:

- **Left column:** heat, filters and matches. The heat layer is summed up in one line ("1,789 kills · up to 29 per cell"); the ⓘ beside it opens the details. The filters fold away under a heading that counts the chosen chips, and the match list takes all the height that is left.
- **Map:** the legend is a 200 px card at the bottom left that folds to a chip. At Fit the map leaves room for it: the fit reads each minimap's land and moves the map right or up, shrinking it only when moving is not enough, so that no land lies under the card. The read-out of x, z and the scale shows only while the pointer is over the map or the map has the keyboard focus.
- **Right panel:** the open match's day, recorded span, humans and bots, and every marker, then its journeys. × and Escape close the panel and keep the match open; "Journeys (N)" on the map or the match's own row brings it back; "Close match" goes back to all matches. From 1200 px the panel has a column of its own and the map narrows; below 1200 px it floats over the map's right edge, ends above the zoom buttons, and the fitted map keeps room for it too.
- **First visit:** the filters are expanded and the legend is open; after that the page remembers how the viewer left them.
- **Unchanged:** the address and its fields, Back and Forward, playback, heat layers, filters, keys, the Data and keys panel. The panel's open state is not part of the address.

**Method.** Exact window sizes were measured in headless Chrome (Playwright from its cache, no new dependency) on a production build of the branch served by `vite preview`; the final measurements and every screenshot use the build of `1421af4`. Real clicks, keys, Back and Forward, Copy link and playback ran in Claude in Chrome on the build of `6cf7f06`, and the view, zoom and playback again on `1421af4`, after the fit changed.
